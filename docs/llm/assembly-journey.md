## Scientific representation contract

- Ordinary atomic material is the bounded opening scope. This is not a complete
  account of every particle, phenomenon or form of matter. Up/down quarks and
  electrons are the useful initial matter identities; electrons are leptons.
  Include relevant interactions; do not march through every Standard Model entry.
- Elementary particles are supplied starting ingredients under the current
  evidence boundary. Do not invent sub-quark or electron interiors to satisfy
  “everything is assembly.” Composition and relationships begin the assembly.
- The proton's valence count is not its whole structure. Gluon/sea dynamics are
  essential; animated strands are a representation, not literal strings or glue.
  Explain that distinction once in context and make it retrievable.
- Gluons mediate the strong interaction; photons mediate electromagnetism.
  Do not depict a gluon as the reason electrons bind to nuclei. Do not switch
  a fundamental interaction on by operating a control.
- Do not portray a proton-to-neutron comparison as casual physical conversion.
  If comparison is included, show separately identified models or explain the
  different valence compositions; do not invent a flavor-changing gesture.
- Hydrogen-1 has zero neutrons. Its electron density is a distribution for one
  bound electron, not many electrons or a planet trajectory.
- Use color only with a consistent stated role, supported by names/shapes.
  QCD color is not literal visible red/green/blue. Probability samples, field
  marks and actual constituent counts are different representation types.
- The camera can pass among representations of composite relationships. Do not
  imply a physical craft traveling inside an elementary particle or show an
  unexplained continuous scale from hydrogen to silicon, a cell or a phone.
- Biology needs chemistry, membranes, energy and reviewed function, not bolt-like
  assembly or a claim to have solved the origin of life. AI routes also need
  algorithms, data, computation, training/inference and evaluation; assembling
  its hardware is not the whole explanation of an AI system.

## Interactions: connection, construction, operation

Use one primary action at a time and large, forgiving targets. A possible
vocabulary is:

1. Focus a part/connection: camera and explanation reveal why it matters.
2. Scrub the assembly: move an authored representation between readable states.
   This may reverse visually without erasing valid committed progress or
   physically undoing an irreversible process. Every state has a declared type.
3. Complete a compatible relationship: validate domain state, animate its
   consequence, then persist completion. No wrong configuration is accepted.
4. Operate the system: switch a circuit, turn a mechanism, or change a reviewed
   biological condition. Choose the interaction from the mechanism, not a
   universal drag-and-drop mechanic.

Foundation gestures can be brief scaffolding. Interactivity in later mechanisms
must expose a causal difference; a prettier completion animation is insufficient.
Avoid point collection, precision puzzles and repeated assembly of quantities.

## Minimum Rosenblatt-owned engine capabilities

These contracts are proposed for the study, not a completed engine or canonical
Course schema. Keep scientific validity and durable progress outside Three.js.

| Capability | Contract and bounded implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Construction model | Typed identities, parts, relations, valid actions and committed state; read-only preview states are distinguishable. A rendered animation cannot manufacture completion. |
| Presentation timeline | Authored beats reference semantic actions/relations. Parts, effects, copy and cues share one clock; interruptions settle safely and replay is deterministic. |
| Camera direction | Explicit travel, inspect and return modes; one camera writer at a time. Connection/part targets, clear silhouettes, occlusion handling, close approaches and readable focus. Manual touch owns inspection until an explicit return. |
| Scale transitions | Bounded local coordinate frames and authored scale bridges, preserving meaning. Keep assembly at each frame's origin; do not reintroduce the previous depth-drift bug or one enormous imprecise world. |
| Input routing | One owner per pointer sequence; semantic picking, forgiving constraints, cancel/background/resize handling, tap alternatives and accessibility actions. No camera/assembly control fights. |
| Representation assets | Named geometry/material/field/probability roles and versions, connection anchors, LODs and a meaning registry. Unknown decorative points do not carry required learning. |
| Behavior and cues | Domain-specific behavior models emit semantic events. Effects/haptics/optional audio respond to those events; a glow does not prove a physical simulation. |
| Rendering adapter | Contained Three.js camera/mesh/shader/animation/post-processing operations. Exit path: replace the adapter without changing authored relations, action validity or progress. |

The current project already has Three.js, OrbitControls, shader materials,
EffectComposer/bloom, procedural geometry, state tests, an offline native shell,
native haptics and retained original audio assets. It does not have the proposed
director, reusable timeline, input arbitration, asset semantics or general
functional simulation layer. The current `main.js` couples those concerns.

Stage the work: storyboard and vocabulary → isolated camera/input study → one
reviewed functional scene → asset pass → phone review through TestFlight.
Promote only reusable capabilities demonstrated by that slice. Do not build an
editor, generic physics world, full universe or all destinations first.

Three.js already supplies camera transforms, animation playback and effects.
This supports the feasibility of a contained study, not a measured claim about
all future mobile scenes. If profiling shows the renderer prevents the authored
result, evaluate a lower-level backend, such as Metal, against the same contracts
and a replacement test. Unity/Unreal/Godot adoption is not implied or selected;
the accepted native-engine direction remains in force.

## Verification and release gates

- First test: can the viewer explain what the initial symbols and interaction
  marks stand for, and why the next composite is different? Completion alone
  is not understanding evidence. Record confusion and incorrect interpretations.
- On the physical iPhone, test single/multi-touch, UI overlap, manual interruption,
  camera return, backgrounding, resizing, safe areas and one-hand reach. Do not
  close the orbit issue from browser mouse inspection alone.
- Profile frame times (including tail behavior), input response, memory and
  thermal behavior. Aim for 60fps with explicit quality options; measure before
  claiming the target is met. Asset detail and bloom are costs, not free quality.
- Reduced motion uses readable static compositions/cuts; explanation remains
  available without motion, color, sound or a held gesture. Timed text must be
  retrievable without restoring a permanent information toolbar.
- Check invalid actions, preview/commit separation, interruptions and persistence.
  New authored semantics require explicit version compatibility/migration;
  preserve current v1/v2 snapshots and completed work.
- Review the functional scene's complete electrical model and part values before
  calling it a validated circuit. No missing material dependency is marked done.
- Creator review determines whether the study has the intended feel. Follow 0009
  for any installable revision: retained identity, new build number, archive,
  TestFlight; no direct installation or automatic public release.
