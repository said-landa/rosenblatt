## Decision

Machines is a connected chain of construction from the deepest scientifically
supported ingredients to natural and technological functional systems. The
learner encounters and constructs the constituents required by a later system
before those constituents become reusable inputs. The nuclear-matter path starts
with quarks, gluons and their interactions. Other required fundamental inputs,
including electrons and electromagnetic interactions, join the shared foundation.

Branching is real: destinations such as a CPU, computer, battery or car have
prerequisite paths through the same construction graph. Completing a shared
process once makes it reusable across later paths, with voluntary inspection or
replay. A learner does not repeat a completed foundation for each destination.
Quantity repetition remains excluded from the intended experience.

## Why now

The creator confirms that “from scratch” means constructive dependency depth.
The prior cosmic-expansion presentation and lever-first recommendation did not
satisfy this direction. Assembly and functional understanding must govern the
opening and every later advance.

## Alternatives considered

- Narrate cosmic chronology: insufficient without meaningful construction.
- Begin with a prepared macroscopic material: skips its required constituents.
- Require every repeated atom or manufacturing quantity: substitutes repetition
  for understanding and conflicts with the process-once direction.
- Invent smaller parts beneath the evidence boundary: scientifically unsupported.

## Consequences

- Separate constituent dependency, formation/production process and historical
  chronology. One arrow cannot silently stand for all three.
- The journey is a dependency graph: branches can share prerequisites and meet
  again. The learner may see a tree-like projection of available destinations.
- Each assembly must reveal a structural or functional relationship, preserve
  authored validity and expose the prerequisites of the next system.
- Primitive ingredients have a declared evidence boundary. Current physics has
  not established smaller constituents of quarks/electrons. Quantum fields
  describe the particles; they are not an invented inventory of smaller pieces.
- Quarks and gluons alone do not supply an electron or all atomic interactions.
  The foundation must include parallel prerequisites where the domain needs them.
- “Completed once” records traversal; it does not prove understanding. Checks
  must obtain separate reasoning evidence.
- Exact materials, nuclear formation pathways, production processes, quantum
  representations and later destination variants require authored research.
  This record is not authorization to approximate unknown physics as fact.
