## Proposed decision

Expand Machines from independently entered machine Courses to a shared journey
from cosmic origins through natural systems and technological capabilities,
with later construction branches. Experience core processes once, then permit
abstraction and voluntary replay. Retain workshop assembly.

The creator proposes a complete free journey without ads and paid additional
branches or an all-branches subscription. The creator explicitly confirmed the no-ad policy on 2026-10-05, including
the free experience. Other commercial terms remain unresolved.

## Why now

The creator wants the materials, energy, tools and production capabilities
behind an artifact to become part of learning, rather than providing all parts
ready-made. The proposed starting point is now cosmic origins, earlier than
prehistoric human tools.

## Alternatives considered

- Keep standalone Courses: smaller scope but misses the requested connected origins.
- Start with prehistoric tools: easier bounded experience but begins after much of
  the requested story; useful as a later era.
- Require exhaustive cosmic and industrial reconstruction: literal breadth
  risks repetition and unsupported science. Prefer representative core processes.

## Constituent construction correction — confirmed 2026-10-11

The creator explicitly rejects the human-invention interpretation of “first
machine.” Natural entities that perform a function belong in the intended
journey. Constituents must become understandable through 3D construction before
they are treated as available materials for a later mechanism. A lever supplied
with unexplained material does not meet this starting requirement. The earlier
lever recommendation is withdrawn as an opening proposal; its research is only
historical context. Cosmic history as passive revelation is also insufficient.

Known dependency structure:
- Quarks and gluons participate in the composite structure of protons/neutrons.
- Nuclear constituents form nuclei; a proton itself is the hydrogen-1 nucleus.
- Nuclei bind electrons to form neutral atoms under appropriate conditions.
- Atoms participate in molecules or extended structures; materials and living
  systems then require their own reviewed intermediate construction paths.
This is not a claim that all materials are molecular or that all systems form
in a single linear chronological sequence. Fundamental ingredients and forces
are parallel prerequisites, not a chain that makes electrons out of protons.

Current physics treats quarks/electrons as elementary, with no experimentally
established smaller constituents. Quantum field theory describes particles as
excitations of fields; those fields are not a set of experimentally identified
smaller mechanical pieces. The educational boundary is the deepest supported
model, not proof that unknown origins or deeper structure cannot exist. No
known creation-from-nothing recipe, or unique historically first natural
Machine, is asserted. “Function” for a natural system refers to behavior and
relationships, not evidence of a designer or assigned cosmic purpose.

Proposed bounded opening: establish the elementary ingredients and relevant
interactions, then construct a proton representation, then neutral hydrogen.
The proton has two up and one down valence quarks plus essential gluon and
quark-antiquark dynamics; three fixed colored beads are not the complete model.
The hydrogen electron is a quantum bound state, not a planet on a fixed orbit.
Representational colors/surfaces must have explicit meaning. Composition
manipulation must not pretend to be a physically executable reaction pathway.
Any field-excitation interaction must respect the selected model's conserved
quantities and confinement; arbitrary isolated-quark production is not accepted.
A first composite milestone is a teaching choice, not proof that protons formed
before every other composite particle. This proposal is unimplemented and
requires creator authorship of the exact interaction before installation.
