# 0010 — Assembly Journey Experience

- Status: Accepted requirements; bounded implementation authorized as Experimental on 2026-10-23
- Date: 2026-10-21
- Decided by: Creator (requirements and rejection of current presentation)
- Refines: 0008; preserves 0002, 0003 and 0009

## Decision

The current particle presentation does not meet the creator's intended
experience. Preserve the shared constituent foundation, but redesign its
presentation as brief, understandable assembly connected to technological and
biological destinations. Brief contextual messages should explain what appears,
then leave the scene. Camera travel, meaningful interaction, better assets,
animation and effects must serve visible relationships and function.

The creator explicitly permits an iterative planning process without rushing.
On 2026-10-23 the creator selected the concrete proposed foundation-plus-circuit
study with “do it”. This authorizes its bounded implementation and normal
TestFlight delivery, not acceptance of the eventual experience quality, a complete
Course or a different engine backend.

## Why now

The creator reports weak “Drag to orbit” behavior and colored particle marks
whose meanings are unclear. Labels such as “Gluons / Strong interaction” are
insufficient explanation. Repeated simple drag/place actions and prolonged
individual-particle inspection do not fulfill the desired journey. Engineering
tests and the delivered TestFlight package do not establish experience quality.

## Alternatives and recommendation

1. Continue isolated particle inspection and improve its polish. Retains the
   familiar implementation but does not answer the broader experience feedback.
2. Make a continuous cinematic introduction. Can establish purpose and travel,
   but alone would not fulfill meaningful learner construction and operation.
3. Briefly establish the shared foundation through composition, then branch into
   a working system with contextual actions and guided exploration. Recommended
   as a bounded study; authorized on 2026-10-23. Timing remains a pacing hypothesis.

## Consequences

- Show the reason for the foundation and the paths ahead early. Retain shared
  prerequisite depth and process reuse; do not pretend that all later paths exist.
- Make visual encodings understandable. Decorative colors or points cannot
  silently represent constituents, forces and probability simultaneously.
- Fundamental particles remain starting ingredients at the current scientific
  evidence boundary. Do not invent smaller parts or physical interiors. Assembly
  concerns supported composite relationships, not an unknown creation recipe.
- Make interactivity mechanism-specific and phone-appropriate. Camera movement
  should reveal the relationship being assembled and yield safely to inspection.
- Design and test at least one functioning engineering or biological subsystem,
  not only a set of rotatable objects. Its research, structure and behavior remain
  creator-owned under 0002.
- Preserve the native engine direction. Build experience-driven camera, input,
  timeline, behavior and effects contracts before broad engine infrastructure.
  Lower-level renderer replacement needs actual evidence, not feature marketing.
- Asset generation is available as an enabling tool; accuracy and artistic
  acceptance still require review. Creating concept art is not producing a
  verified, traversable 3D asset.
- Sound is a possible future resource, not automatically restored in the current
  app. No public release or direct development installation is implied.

## Evidence and uncertainty

The creator's report establishes rejection of the current experience. Source
inspection confirms arbitrary ingredient colors, point-based particle glyphs,
a geometric gluon knot, single-center camera orbits and a monolithic scene loop.
It does not isolate the reported physical touch issue. No physical gesture fix
or measured performance conclusion is claimed.

The paired [human proposal](../human/assembly-journey.md) and
[engineering plan](../llm/assembly-journey.md) contain the concrete recommended
study and its scientific/verification boundaries. One generated visual direction
study explores an interior electronics viewpoint. It is not an approved lesson
asset, verified phone design or app implementation.
