Rosenblatt is a chain of construction from the deepest scientifically supported
ingredients to natural and technological functional systems. The learner
constructs the prerequisites of later materials and mechanisms rather than
receiving unexplained ready-made inputs. Inspectable 3D assembly and functional
understanding govern the journey. Cosmic narration alone does not fulfill it.

## Shared foundation and branches

Quarks/gluons begin the nuclear-matter path. Electrons and electromagnetic
interactions are parallel prerequisites for atoms. The foundation then supports
reviewed molecular or extended structures, materials and larger systems.
The model is a dependency graph: branches can share prerequisites and meet again.
It is not one universal chronological or manufacturing recipe.

Experience each core process once. Subsequent uses can simplify quantity while
preserving inspection and voluntary replay. Shared completion carries across
branches toward a CPU, computer, battery, car or other selected destination.
Exact destination variants and intermediate processes remain to be researched.

[Initial dependency map](../../Courses/constituent-construction/dependency-map.md):
the first proposed complete path establishes fundamental ingredients, constructs
a proton model, then neutral hydrogen. The first nuclear branch also leads toward
a neutron model and reviewed larger nuclei. The Lab candidate now lets the learner
place wave-like constituent symbols, reveal binding and explore a hydrogen 1s
probability cloud. Exact gestures and representations await creator acceptance.
A proton is a teaching milestone, not a
claim that it was uniquely the first natural Machine ever to exist.

## Scientific and interaction boundary

Current evidence has not established smaller constituents of quarks/electrons.
Start at the deepest supported model and make that limit visible; do not invent
subcomponents. Quantum fields describe the elementary particles rather than
providing a known smaller mechanical inventory. Natural function describes
behavior and relationships, without implying an intentionally assigned purpose.

Reviewed construction paths protect valid state. Incorrect arrangements cannot
be accepted, saved as successful or carried forward. The learner can inspect,
rotate, zoom and try interactions. English copy remains minimal;
visual relationships and behavior carry the learning experience.
