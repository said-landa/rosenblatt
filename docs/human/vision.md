## Creator clarification — 2026-10-11

Machines includes natural functional systems, not only human inventions. The
learner should construct the constituents needed by later systems: understand
and assemble matter before treating a material as an available part. The
immediate question is the deepest scientifically supported starting layer and
first composite assembly, not a lever or an archaeological first invention.
Every advance must involve inspectable 3D structure and functional relationships.
Cosmic narration alone does not fulfill that construction purpose.

The Origin implementation and mouse work remain retained. The new constituent
candidate replaces only the Lab packaging entry. It is a composition model, not
a physical formation recipe or an accepted canonical Course. Record 0005 remains the separate accepted native
reference selection. See [the origin record](origin-journey.md).

## Mission

Help people understand the machines that shape the modern world by constructing
them through interactive learning experiences.

The goal is not isolated theory or memorized terminology. The goal is durable
intuition: after a course, a learner should be able to explain how the machine's
parts cooperate to produce its function.

## Core experience

A learner begins with a question and a deliberately incomplete machine. Each
new component:

- answers a visible question;
- introduces only the knowledge needed at that moment;
- reveals a functional relationship;
- advances the machine toward a coherent whole.

Learning becomes construction rather than passive observation.

## Product promise

Each course should leave the learner able to:

- recognize the machine's important components;
- explain why they exist;
- explain the essential relationships between them;
- reason about what would change if a component were absent or different;
- see the real machine as an intelligible system rather than a black box.

## Character of the experience

Machines should feel clear, curious, active, rigorous, and quietly excellent.
Visual craft serves understanding. Interaction serves reasoning. The tutor
serves the learner's current situation.

## Boundaries

Machines prioritizes structure, function, and relationships through assembly.
It is not primarily a professional physics simulator, a video-course platform,
a generic game, or a chat interface placed around ordinary content.

## Current vision question

Which first machine can express this promise completely at a small enough scope
to investigate deeply?
