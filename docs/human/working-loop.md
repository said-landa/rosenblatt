# Working Loop

> Detailed counterpart: [LLM working-loop protocol](../llm/working-loop.md)

The collaboration should continually convert uncertainty into a decision,
experiment, implementation, or verified lesson.

Most creator time should remain inside Machine, Course, and learner-experience
authorship. Engineering work runs around that creative loop without requiring a
creator decision packet for ordinary implementation.

## Responsibility routing

- Creator-owned work becomes a concise decision packet, prototype, authoring
  action, or learner-experience review.
- LLM-owned work is implemented and verified autonomously within accepted
  boundaries, then reported concisely.
- Shared seams require only the smallest creator decision that the LLM cannot
  responsibly make.

## The loop

1. **Orient.** Begin from [Current Project Position](status.md).
2. **Route responsibility.** Decide whether the next work belongs to you, the
   LLM, or a shared seam.
3. **Choose one decision or action.** Select the work that unlocks the most
   useful evidence or construction.
4. **Decide only when needed.** For creator-owned or escalated choices, the LLM
   presents a compact packet; ordinary engineering proceeds without one.
5. **Record and propagate.** The LLM updates the decision ledger and every
   affected document or contract.
6. **Build or investigate.** Produce the smallest complete artifact capable of
   testing the decision.
7. **Verify and learn.** Compare the result with explicit acceptance criteria.
8. **Set the next decision.** Update the current position before ending the
   cycle.

## Shape of a decision packet

The LLM should give you:

- the decision in one sentence;
- why it is needed now;
- two to four serious options;
- a recommendation and its reasoning;
- what is known, inferred, and still uncertain;
- consequences, reversibility, and cost of being wrong;
- the concrete work or evidence unlocked by the decision.

You can always ask for the rigorous layer, but it should not be required for an
ordinary decision.

## Forward motion

An imperfect reversible decision can be more valuable than indefinite planning
when it produces evidence. An irreversible or identity-defining decision
deserves more scrutiny. The LLM must state which kind is being proposed.

Planning is complete for the moment when the next useful action is concrete and
its result can change what we believe.
