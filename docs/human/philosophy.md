# Engineering Philosophy

> Detailed counterpart: [LLM engineering philosophy](../llm/philosophy.md)

Machines is intended to be understood, corrected, and extended across decades.

## Foundational commitments

- Domain truth comes before production.
- Educational understanding comes before visual spectacle.
- Concepts come before technologies.
- Internal tools are first-class products.
- Project-critical formats and data remain under project control.
- Consequential decisions preserve reasoning and evidence.
- Abstractions are discovered through real courses rather than speculation.
- Replaceable parts remain separated from durable foundations.
- A solution is not accepted merely because it works.
- Every investment in depth must serve a purpose.

## Practical meaning

Depth is not the accumulation of features. It is the removal of unexamined
assumptions until we can explain why a system exists, what it governs, how it
can fail, and how its result can be verified.

Fast experiments are useful when they produce evidence. Durable foundations
are useful when evidence has shown what must endure. They should be named and
judged differently.
