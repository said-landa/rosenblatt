## What is implemented

- Destination sculptures introduce phones, satellites, cells and computing.
- Labeled up/down symbols compose a simplified proton model; strong-interaction
  strands have a declared symbolic role. The independent electron then leads to
  a stationary hydrogen-1 1s probability cloud, explicitly one electron.
- Five-second cancellable assembly previews have a range control, keyboard and
  tap alternative; a distinct confirmation saves only completed model reviews.
- Contextual copy fades after 14 active seconds and can be reopened. Footnotes
  open complete explanations. No sound, pause or mute controls.
- The path map shows shared atomic matter and future directions. Replay is
  voluntary. Electronics enters an explicitly separate manufactured-parts study.
- Battery, resistor, LED and switch descend on coordinated paths. Completing
  the preview enables a real open/closed series-loop model and readable current.
- Camera approaches parts at board level; drag claims inspection until Return
  to journey. Native/background interruption stops assembly playback. Reduced
  motion uses cuts and instant preview completion with a separate confirmation.

## Owned contracts and replacement path

`model.js` owns validity, progress and the bounded circuit model. `director.js`
owns camera authority and paths using numeric arrays. Both run without Three.js.
`renderer.js` is a replaceable Three.js adapter: procedural illustrative geometry,
materials, projected labels, camera transforms and rendering. `main.js` binds DOM
input and clocks to those contracts. No general game engine or canonical Course
schema is adopted. This study does not supply the whole native engine.

The only new storage key is `rosenblatt.assembly-journey.v1`. It records explicit
proton/hydrogen **model reviews**, not Course or material prerequisites. Existing
constituent, origin and mouse progress keys are untouched. Circuit operation is
session-local and earns no Course progress. Storage failure remains usable and
is announced. Older builds retain their source and storage namespaces.

## Scientific and electrical boundaries

Symbols and field strands are illustrative; no free-quark assembly, elementary
particle interiors or QCD computation. Proton valence `uud` omits essential sea
and field dynamics explained in the retrievable note. Electron density is sampled
from the isotropic 1s radial distribution, Gamma(3, a0/2), not an orbit or tracks.
Hydrogen-1 has zero neutrons. Nuclear and atomic sizes are not to scale.

The circuit uses 3.0 V, 220 Ω, a fixed 2.0 V red-LED drop and an ideal switch.
`I = max(0, (V − Vf) / R)` only for the complete correctly oriented closed series
loop; otherwise current and LED emission are zero. Resistor dissipation at the
nominal operating point is 4.55 mW. This is a bounded DC behavioral model, not a
nonlinear component-specific simulator. Geometry is an illustrative two-cell
holder, axial resistor, LED and hinged switch, not a reviewed manufacturing kit.
The resistor bands are red/red/brown/gold (220 Ω, ±5%); nominal values are modeled.
The holder has alternating cells and a series strap. No traversed atom-to-board
manufacturing or material prerequisites are claimed.

Sources checked: [CERN Standard Model](https://home.web.cern.ch/science/physics/standard-model/)
and [Adafruit, Circuits from Scratch](https://learn.adafruit.com/lets-put-leds-in-things/from-scratch)
(2026-10-23); prior scientific source audit is in
[the engineering specification](../../../../docs/llm/assembly-journey.md).
All runtime assets are local code-generated geometry/canvas textures. The earlier
AI concept image is not packaged or presented as engineering evidence.
