The image was generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool and visually
inspected. It explores a low viewpoint within an exploded electronics assembly:
tactile surfaces, foreground depth, matching connection regions and room for
camera travel. No external image or commercial design was supplied as reference.

It is a proposed artistic direction. The IC package, footprint, board routing
and vias are not verified against a particular phone or component datasheet.
It is not a working circuit, scientific diagram, 3D mesh or app screenshot.
Do not import it as technical truth or substitute it for traversable geometry.
The current app bundle does not reference it; no new version is built/uploaded.

Generation used a portrait, tactile 3D engineering concept prompt: a tiny moving
viewpoint between an IC and its circuit-board footprint in an exploded assembly,
with copper traces, vias and clear depth; no UI, text, rainbow dots, starfield,
glowing electrical streams, cockpit or characters. The full art-direction brief
is preserved in the prompt file. Generated source:
`exec-0fd2b89a-89a8-40f2-970f-3e0ea9db7979.png`.
