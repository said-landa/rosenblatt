Asset type: one visual direction study for an immersive engineering assembly journey; a concept image, not a scientific diagram, final app screen or verified phone design.

Scene and subject: a close, ground-level view along a small section of a circuit board. The camera is between the board and the component, looking toward their connection. Preserve a coherent component and corresponding pad layout. Show enough nearby board structure to understand what is being joined; leave breathing room.
Style: beautifully crafted 3D engineering concept render, tactile and precise, imaginative in viewpoint rather than in invented hardware. Restrained material differences, realistic metal and ceramic surfaces, fine manufacturing detail, warm contact reflections and cool shaded cavities. A sense of discovery inside something real.
Composition: portrait composition, strong foreground/middle-ground/background parallax, a clear focal connection and an inviting route for a future camera move. The real electronics occupy the view; no spacecraft cockpit or characters.
Constraints: no UI, no text, no typography, no logo, no watermark, no rainbow dots, no starfield, no cosmic scenery, no glowing electric streams, no invented scientific labels, no claim that the electronics are already powered. This is an exploded representation for assembly, not levitating hardware in normal use.
